    04 / HONEST BOUNDARIES

    Designed for real autonomy.
    Validated in simulation.

    Understanding the current scope is part of trusting the platform. The current fleet has two mobile carriers and one humanoid navigator. All three support simulated navigation, stopping and waiting; physical manipulation and balancing are outside this scope.

    Available in the simulator

    Mission workflows, movement permits, stops, environment revalidation, learned routing, evidence, recovery and mixed-fleet navigation have documented test coverage.

    Configuration or validation needed

    Model planning, vision, voice, external lookup and tracing paths depend on suitable credentials, services and live validation. Operator review and replan-and-hold are available; approvals cannot override hard safety limits.

    Not yet delivered

    Physical humanoid manipulation and balancing, cross-platform skill transfer, enforced role-specific task eligibility and multi-site failover are outside current implementation.
